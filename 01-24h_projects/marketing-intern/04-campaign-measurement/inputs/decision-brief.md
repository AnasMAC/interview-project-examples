# Decision handoff and experiment constraints

**D1 — unverified summary.** “Search clearly won. It had 420 sessions, and qualified requests can be added to completed requests to count conversions. Newsletter cost was 100, so its performance must be worse. We can safely double all spend.” This is a flawed draft to audit, not evidence. Do not copy its numbers without checking.

**D2 — decision constraints.** One next experiment only. Maximum synthetic acquisition budget is **EUR 120**. It may use search or newsletter, changing either landing CTA wording or eligibility explanation, not both. You may allocate less than the cap or reserve budget until tracking is fixed. No ads or messages are actually sent. No historical performance is guaranteed to repeat.

**D3 — evaluation plan.** Set a two-week simulated observation window with the same seven-day conversion/qualification window after the last included session. Define numerator, denominator, guardrail, and a simple decision rule. A minimum sample/insufficient-data fallback is needed, but no advanced statistics or statistically powered test is required. Do not invent certainty from small samples. The experiment can compare to a contemporaneous control or be explicitly exploratory; disclose limits.

**D4 — operations.** Ada receives a source-record anomaly list; Bo receives campaign/experiment recommendations. The worksheet should preserve raw evidence and show all exclusions. Do not infer revenue, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, causal campaign superiority, or product effectiveness from demo-request data.
