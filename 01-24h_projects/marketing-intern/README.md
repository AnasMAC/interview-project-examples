# Marketing Intern: AI-enabled 24-hour exercises

Assign **one** package to each candidate. Each package is a self-contained fictional assessment. The candidate's 24 hours begin when they click Start on the link in the invitation email, which also unlocks the package zip; they are not expected to work the full 24 hours. No AI tool is provided; no paid tools, accounts, outside research, production access, publishing, advertising, or real outreach are needed. The clock tests bounded delivery and follow-through, not sleep sacrifice or availability.

Export only the assigned package's `candidate/` directory, preserving its local inputs. Do not export `evaluator/` or this role index. Staff launch instructions and ground truth are in each package's evaluator guide. **Evaluator files are visible to repository readers; this exercise bank is not a confidential test.** Do not rely on secrecy; each evaluator guide includes an optional follow-up question that can check ownership if the candidate is invited to a later interview.

Send the invitation email with the start link; core work alone can earn full marks. Score all packages consistently: usable result 40%, accuracy/verification 25%, prioritization 20%, communication/handoff 15%. Do not score hours worked, AI-detector output, personal tool access, or sheer content volume.

All company names, research observations, product capabilities, prices, and metrics in this role bank are synthetic. They are authoritative only for their assessment; they make no claims about Ostorlab or real products.
