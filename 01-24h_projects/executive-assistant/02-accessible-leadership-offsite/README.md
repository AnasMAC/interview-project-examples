# Accessible leadership offsite

You support the leadership team of fictional Harbor Signal. On the snapshot date **2027-03-10**, you inherit an incomplete plan for an eight-person offsite on **2027-03-18**. Produce a bookable proposal within policy, without making bookings. The goal is a full-day accessible meeting, a reconciled all-in budget, and a practical contingency handoff.

## Supplied inputs and setup

Read [brief and inherited draft](inputs/brief.md), [attendees](inputs/attendees.csv), and [supplier and travel options](inputs/options.md). Use only supplied fixed offsets and prices; no real maps, venues, or travel research are required. A text editor and calculator/free spreadsheet suffice. Baseline skills: event coordination, budget arithmetic, reading quotes, and accessible participant communication.

## Core work and deliverables

1. Recommend a venue, named travel options for each traveler, and any hotel/transfer required. Show dates, arrival times, headcount, dietary/access provisions, approvals, and confirmation tasks. Compare all three venues against mandatory requirements.
2. Create an updateable line-item budget with quantities, unit prices, tax basis, base total, 10% contingency, total, cap/headroom, and source IDs. Include spreadsheet formulas or an explicit calculation recipe so a colleague can rerun it after a price or headcount change. Demonstrate a cross-check of headcount, tax and total against the supplied sources. Explain corrections to the inherited draft. EUR throughout; round money to cents.
3. Provide a day agenda, a dated preparation/booking checklist with owners, and a disruption fallback. Assign unresolved supplier checks rather than invent confirmation.
4. Draft two unsent messages: a participant briefing (arrival instructions, day outline, private needs channel) and a venue clarification/hold request (capacity, access, meals, cost/terms).
5. Add the log, AI-use note, and walkthrough below.

Observable acceptance: all eight attendees counted; onsite goal and 09:00 arrival requirement respected; accessible room and transport chosen from supplied facts; dietary needs routed safely; no capacity or time contradiction; tax and contingency correctly applied; cost below EUR 3,600 or a clear escalation rather than silent overspend; nothing booked without authority. Separate public participant information from the restricted organizer needs list.

Optional stretch: show the budget sensitivity if attendance drops by one, without assuming cancellation rights.
