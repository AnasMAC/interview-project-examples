# Executive inbox and calendar recovery

You support Mira Haddad, CEO of the fictional security software company Harbor Signal. At the scenario snapshot, **2027-03-08 08:30 UTC+01:00**, Mira has conflicting calendar entries, a renewal at risk, and administrative requests. Prepare a workable day and a controlled inbox handoff; you cannot obtain new replies during the exercise.

## Supplied inputs and setup

Open [operating rules](inputs/operating-rules.md), [calendar export](inputs/calendar.csv), and [inbox](inputs/inbox.md). IDs are stable evidence references. Dates contain fixed UTC offsets: use the supplied offsets rather than assumptions about real cities or daylight saving. No calendar import or code is needed. Baseline skills: professional correspondence, calendar planning, prioritization, and basic time conversion.

## Core work and deliverables

1. Create an inbox tracker for **all 10 messages**: message ID, priority and reason, owner, next action, due time with offset, status, and source. A proposal requiring approval must remain pending.
2. Supply a revised day schedule for Mira showing start/end with offset, attendee/delegate, event ID, status, and a change reason. Include working blocks, lunch, and external-call buffers. Show the changed external meetings in both participants' offsets and list unresolved dependencies.
3. Draft exactly **four** unsent messages: customer renewal correction; partner reschedule; a cautious payment response; an executive digest of at most 250 words. Include recipient roles and the decision/request in each.
4. Create a reusable calendar/inbox validation sheet or procedure (spreadsheet formulas, a no-code checklist/filter method, or a simple local script). Demonstrate checks for CEO overlaps, offsets, external buffers, focus total/block length, and missing inbox owners/deadlines. A colleague must be able to rerun it after a schedule change; programming and integrations are optional.
5. Include the log, AI-use note, and walkthrough specified below.

Observable acceptance: every inbox item has a disposition; the fixed treasury meeting and lunch survive; no overlapping CEO commitments; at least 90 minutes of focus in blocks of at least 30 minutes; each external call has 15 minutes clear immediately before and after; calendar changes use the latest explicit message where it conflicts with the export; payment is handled within authority; sensitive personnel information is restricted. Show validation results and the repeatable checking method, including zero-issue results when appropriate. Give proposed appointments, not fabricated confirmations. You may defer noncritical items if the tracker states who will handle them and when.

Stop when the core is usable. Optional stretch: describe a reusable inbox view, without implementing integrations.
