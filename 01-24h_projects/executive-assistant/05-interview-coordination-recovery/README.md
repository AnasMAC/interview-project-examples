You coordinate a fictional Harbor Signal hiring round for four applicants. At snapshot **2027-03-25 09:00 UTC+01**, an inherited schedule has conflicts and outdated availability. Prepare a workable two-day interview plan, fair candidate communications, and a repeatable validation handoff. You are coordinating interviews, not evaluating or selecting applicants.

## Core work and deliverables

1. Produce a proposed schedule for **eight interviews**: four 30-minute screens with Jo and four 45-minute panels with both Mira and Tess. Show interview ID, stage, candidate, interviewer(s), date, start/end in company and candidate offsets, and status/source.
2. Create a coordination tracker covering each candidate's availability/hold, invitation status, private accommodation action, panel preparation, feedback follow-up, owner, due timestamp, dependency and next check. Keep screening/feedback evidence distinct from fabricated completed activity.
3. Supply a reusable schedule check (spreadsheet formulas, no-code checklist/filter method, or script) for overlap, availability, duration, stage order, interviewer buffers and time conversion. Demonstrate it on your final schedule and show at least three corrections to the inherited draft. Staff must be able to rerun the method after one availability change; programming is optional.
4. Draft exactly three unsent messages: Aisha's invitation showing her local times; Dev's private accommodation confirmation/request; a brief internal panel handoff with schedule, preparation and feedback deadlines. Do not disclose accommodations to the full panel unnecessarily.
5. Add the log, AI-use note and walkthrough below.

Observable acceptance: all four candidates receive both stages within supplied availability; screen precedes panel by at least 15 minutes; exact stage durations; no double booking; 15-minute buffer between any two interviews for each interviewer; both mandatory panelists included; candidate offsets correct; all slots are proposed, not claimed accepted; private needs routed under policy; confirmation/feedback follow-through has named owners and due times. You may explain an infeasible plan and seek a specific exception, but the original inputs provide a fully feasible schedule.

Optional stretch: explain how to batch reminders without exposing candidates to each other; no integration required.
