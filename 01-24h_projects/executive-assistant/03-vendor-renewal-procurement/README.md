# Vendor renewal and procurement brief

At the fictional scenario snapshot **2027-03-15 09:00 UTC+01**, Harbor Signal must decide whether to renew its meeting-record platform before a notice deadline. You support the CEO and CFO: normalize the options, expose contract/quote differences, and prepare the decision and approval path. You cannot sign, pay, or contact suppliers.

## Supplied inputs and setup

Read [requirements and authority](inputs/requirements.md), [quote data](inputs/quotes.csv), and [contracts and correspondence](inputs/contract-notes.md). IDs and the defined source hierarchy are sufficient for decisions. No external vendor research is required. Baseline skills: procurement coordination, spreadsheet/calculator use, contract reading, executive decision writing.

## Core work and deliverables

1. Build a reusable comparison table for all three vendors: actual required and billed seats, recurring cost, one-time fees, tax, first-year total, cap/headroom, hard requirements, evidence IDs, and open checks. Include formulas or a clear calculation recipe so staff can update seat count or prices without reconstructing your logic. Show why the inherited totals differ.
2. Recommend one option in a decision brief of at most 500 words. Distinguish factual eligibility, preferred tradeoffs, conditions before approval, and unresolved facts. Include an alternative/fallback.
3. Provide a dated approval/renewal tracker with owner, dependency, due time/offset, status and evidence, including the notice deadline and configuration/export tasks. Create a repeatable check for missing owner/deadline or unapproved commitments and demonstrate it on your tracker.
4. Draft exactly two unsent messages: a quote/contract clarification request to the relevant supplier; an internal approval request to Mira and Ren containing normalized cost and the next decision.
5. Add the log, AI-use note and walkthrough below.

Observable acceptance: all vendor costs normalized for 24 active users over twelve months; minimum seats and VAT applied once; mandatory retention/DPA/export requirements checked against the controlling source; no conditional discount assumed earned; signed terms govern renewal notice; costs fit the cap or require escalation; approvals and tentative confirmations clearly labeled. Do not offer legal conclusions beyond the supplied procurement rules.

Optional stretch: model a 30-user case with the same formulas; full marks require only 24 users.
