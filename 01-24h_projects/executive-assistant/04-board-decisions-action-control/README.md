# Board decisions and action control

You support Mira, chair and CEO of fictional Harbor Signal. At the snapshot **2027-03-23 09:00 UTC+01**, yesterday's board meeting has an unreliable draft summary and a stale action register. Produce a traceable record that staff can use without treating discussion as authorization.

## Supplied inputs and setup

Read [recordkeeping rules](inputs/recordkeeping-rules.md), [meeting notes](inputs/meeting-notes.md), [previous actions](inputs/previous-actions.csv), and [draft summary](inputs/draft-summary.md). These notes are the complete authoritative meeting evidence; no audio, external research, board software, or legal analysis is required. Baseline skills: meeting documentation, spreadsheet tracking, confidentiality, and source reconciliation.

## Core work and deliverables

1. Produce draft minutes (roughly 600–900 words or less if complete) with attendees/quorum, decisions/votes/conditions, discussions without decisions, and next meeting. Cite note/action IDs. Mark chair approval pending.
2. Create a reconciled, updateable action register: original/new ID, action, accountable owner, due timestamp/offset, dependency, status as of snapshot, source, next check. Preserve history for all six prior actions and add all new actions from the meeting. Separate closed evidence, overdue work, and new dependencies; do not silently overwrite deadlines.
3. Provide a repeatable register check/view flagging missing owners/deadlines, overdue open work and blocked dependencies. Use formulas, a no-code filter procedure, or a simple script; demonstrate results on your register so a colleague can rerun at another snapshot.
4. Draft two unsent messages: a board-only chair-approval request with unresolved confirmations; a staff-safe action handoff that excludes restricted HR detail. Include a short discrepancy table identifying and correcting the inherited draft's substantive errors.
5. Add the log, AI-use note and walkthrough below.

Observable acceptance: quorum uses board members only; only recorded votes/agreements become decisions; spending approval retains its release conditions; the open renewal remains a quote; all six prior actions receive an evidenced disposition; owners/deadlines/dependencies are traceable; overdue and blocked work are visible; staff summary does not reveal restricted HR details. Identify uncertainty rather than supplying invented vote counts or approvals.

Optional stretch: propose a monthly action review routine, without building integrations.
