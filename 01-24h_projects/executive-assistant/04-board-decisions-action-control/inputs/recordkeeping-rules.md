# Synthetic board recordkeeping rules

G01 | Authority and quorum
Board members: Mira (chair/CEO), Ren (CFO), Arun (independent director), Ellie (director). Quorum requires at least **three board members present**. Guests may discuss but do not vote. Assistant prepares drafts; chair approval is needed before minutes become the official record. This exercise supplies no real law or governance advice.

G02 | Decisions and money
Record approved motions, vote counts where stated, and conditions. A proposal discussed without a vote/agreement is not an approved action. A board cap is not proof of payment or full immediate release. No email, payment, official filing, or approval occurs during the exercise.

G03 | Action register
Snapshot for overdue checks is **2027-03-23 09:00 UTC+01**. A due timestamp strictly earlier than the snapshot is overdue if not closed; a task exactly due at the snapshot is not yet overdue. Prior IDs/history must survive. A changed date needs a source and prior due date. No vague "team" owner. If a dependency blocks delivery, show it even when the task's nominal due date is future. Closed status needs supplied completion evidence.

G04 | Confidentiality
Board materials are board-only plus explicitly authorized support staff. Named employment/compensation details are restricted to Mira, Jo (People), and the board. Staff handoff may say "People follow-up assigned privately" without identities/details. Do not copy restricted details into the ordinary action register; use a restricted-reference row if necessary. Discussed HR possibilities are not announcements.

G05 | Fictional commercial figures
Aster EUR 84,000 is an **open 12-month renewal quote**, not signed revenue. Base Q2 forecast EUR 310,000 excludes it; including it creates an uncommitted upside scenario EUR 394,000. Do not describe that upside as closed/signed revenue.

G06 | Upcoming dates
All timestamps fixed UTC+01. Next board meeting is 2027-04-05 09:00–10:00. No approval is implied by sending drafts to the chair.
